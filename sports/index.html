The Open Ballot

No committee. No sportswriters' room. FBS football and Division I basketball, ranked by the people who actually watch them.

Sign up once. Every Sunday morning you get a ballot. Fill out your Top 25, and the fan ranking drops Sunday evening, with a short blurb on every team and what its recent form actually looks like.

Vote this week → See the rankings

Weekly cycle: ballot email Sunday 9am PT · ballot closes Sunday 4pm PT · rankings released Sunday 5pm PT. Times are adjustable in one config line.

Demo mode: no Supabase project is connected yet, so votes save on this device only and the rankings below are a sample. Connect Supabase (see supabase-schema.sql) to go live.

    Sign up for the Sunday ballot

    One ballot per person, per sport, per week. Supabase Auth handles sign-in; your email is only used for the Sunday ballot and results, never sold.

    Data: team info, records, schedules and scores via ESPN's public site API (unofficial, no key required). Logos are the schools' own marks, shown via ESPN. Rankings themselves are fan votes, not ESPN's. Scope: FBS football and NCAA Division I men's basketball only.
