The live feed includes every planned week — dinners, leftovers nights, and nights out — as evening events. When either of you changes the plan, the feed updates.

Paste this link into your calendar app:

Apple Calendar

  1. On iPhone: open Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.
  2. Paste the feed link, tap Next, then Save.
  3. On Mac: open Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription, paste the link, then Subscribe.

Google Calendar

  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Settings (gear icon) → Add calendar → From URL.
  2. Paste the feed link and click Add calendar.
