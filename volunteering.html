Volunteering by Austin Zehnder

Austin Zehnder gives back to his local community. He volunteers with Habitat for Humanity and the Salvation Army, coaches youth lacrosse clinics in the Bay Area, and volunteers with San Francisco Parks and Recreation to clean up parks and beaches.
