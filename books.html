Book recommendations by Austin Zehnder

Books worth your time, in alphabetical order by title. More will be added here over time.

All About Love

bell hooks

A clear, honest look at what love actually is, and why it takes work, care, and attention in everyday life.

Atomic Habits

James Clear

Small habits, done consistently, compound. A practical system for building good habits and breaking bad ones.

Freakonomics

Steven D. Levitt and Stephen J. Dubner

Ask a better question, follow the incentives, and the data will often tell a story you did not expect.

Keep Going

Austin Kleon

Short, encouraging advice for staying creative over the long run, especially when the work gets slow or discouraging.

Mindset

Carol S. Dweck

A growth mindset treats ability as something you build, not something you are stuck with. That shift changes how you learn, work, and recover from setbacks.

Moneyball

Michael Lewis

How the Oakland As used overlooked data to compete with far bigger budgets, and why conventional wisdom is often just expensive habit.
