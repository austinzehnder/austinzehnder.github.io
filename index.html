About

I work at the intersection of data, finance, and real estate. With 10+ years in credit analysis, underwriting, and portfolio management, I turn complex housing and financial data into actionable insights. My operations experience runs through real estate: asset management, property management, and lending, where I have managed properties and portfolios, not just modeled them. That work runs through my projects: Austin Zehndra Analytics for home values and comps, Zehndra for money and project templates, and housing and finance writing and visualizations. I am based in San Francisco, and studied Economics and Finance at Centre College.
