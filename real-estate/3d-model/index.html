Custom 3D Property Model

Turn your property and project plans into an interactive 3D model you can explore room by room.

Send your property address, scope of work, and project plans, and you will receive a custom interactive 3D model built from your plans. Walk through the remodel, addition, or ADU before any work starts, and share the model with a contractor, partner, or lender.

Buy on Gumroad → Start your intake →

What you get

  • Interactive 3D model of your property and planned work, viewable in your browser.
  • Before and after views for your scope of work, so you can compare the current home with the plan.
  • Floor plan and room labels where your plans allow.
  • Human reviewed, built from your address, scope, and uploaded plans, not generated from an address alone.

How it works

  • 1. Try the free demo. Open the demo and tap Load demo home to explore a finished model: orbit it, restyle it, and flip before and after. No form, no payment.
  • 2. Buy on Gumroad. Pick the tier that matches your project below, and check out on Gumroad.
  • 3. Submit your project. Go to the 3D intake site and submit your property address, scope of work, and project plans. Use the same email you used at checkout, so your order can be matched to your intake.
  • 4. Get your model by email. Your model is built and delivered manually by email as a link and files, within 3 business days where possible. Turnaround depends on plan completeness and scope.

What it is not

This is a custom visualization based on the plans you provide. It is not an architectural drawing, not an appraisal, and not a permit document. Plans, measurements, and permits remain your responsibility. If you need drawings for permits or construction, work with a licensed architect or designer.
