Real Estate

Two tools for a property you own, are buying, or are planning work on. One prices it. One shows the plan in 3D.

Both are built from the same background: 10+ years in credit analysis, underwriting, and portfolio management, plus hands-on work in asset management, property management, and lending. These are practical tools, not automated guesses.

Home Value Estimator

Get a free brief sales price estimate from the address and what you know about the home, then order a full human-reviewed comp report for $4.99. The full report includes comparable recent sales with adjustments and a recommended price range in a 1-page PDF.

Custom 3D Property Model

Enter your property address, scope of work, and project plans, and get an interactive 3D model you can explore room by room, with before and after views. From $99 for a single room, $179 for a full home, and $249 for an addition or ADU.

Which one do you need?

  • Pricing a home to sell or buy: start with the Home Value Estimator. The free estimate gives you a range in minutes, and the $4.99 full report adds real comps.
  • Planning a remodel, addition, or ADU: start with the Custom 3D Property Model. It turns your plans into a model you can walk through before work starts.
  • Both: price it first, then model the work, so you can see whether the project is worth doing.
