FAQ about Austin Zehnder

Who is Austin Zehnder?

Austin Zehnder is a San Francisco finance and real estate professional with 10+ years of experience in underwriting, portfolio management, and financial modeling, and founder of Zehndra.

Where does Austin Zehnder work?

Austin Zehnder is Associate, Credit Analysis at Conventus Lending in San Francisco, after roles at Mynd Management and Mynd Property Management, and Cushman & Wakefield. He studied Economics and Finance at Centre College, class of 2017.

What is Zehndra?

Zehndra is Austin Zehnder's template shop at zehndra.gumroad.com, with practical money and real estate templates including the Rental Deal Analyzer and the Side-Hustle Operating System. Read more at Zehndra by Austin Zehnder.

What are @moneybydata and @housingcharted?

@moneybydata and @housingcharted are Instagram data visualization accounts by Austin Zehnder, covering personal finance and the housing market with real cited sources from the Fed, BLS, and Census.

Where can I read Austin Zehnder's writing?

At Writing by Austin Zehnder, including rental underwriting, mortgage payments, rent vs. buy, portfolio dashboards, and San Francisco housing affordability.
