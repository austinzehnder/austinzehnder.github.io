FAQ about Austin Zehnder

Who is Austin Zehnder?

I work at the intersection of data, finance, and real estate. With 10+ years in credit analysis, underwriting, and portfolio management, I turn complex housing and financial data into actionable insights. My operations experience runs through real estate: asset management, property management, and lending, where I have managed properties and portfolios, not just modeled them. That work runs through my projects: Austin Zehnder Analytics for home values and comps, Zehndra for money and project templates, and housing and finance writing and visualizations. I am based in San Francisco, and studied Economics and Finance at Centre College.

Where does Austin Zehnder work?

Austin Zehnder is Associate, Credit Analysis at Conventus Lending in San Francisco, after roles at Mynd Management and Mynd Property Management, and Cushman & Wakefield. He studied Economics and Finance at Centre College, class of 2017.

What is Zehndra?

Zehndra is Austin Zehnder's template shop at zehndra.gumroad.com, with practical money and real estate templates including the Rental Deal Analyzer and the Side-Hustle Operating System. Read more at Zehndra by Austin Zehnder.

What is @moneybydata?

@moneybydata is an Instagram data visualization account by Austin Zehnder, covering personal finance with real cited sources from the Fed, BLS, and Census.

Where can I read Austin Zehnder's writing?

At Writing by Austin Zehnder, including rental underwriting, mortgage payments, rent vs. buy, portfolio dashboards, and San Francisco housing affordability.
